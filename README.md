## Research

### AI for Neuroimaging
**Mental Health Mapping Lab · Friedrich Schiller University Jena**

Developing and evaluating machine learning pipelines for brain imaging analysis, particularly **CSF segmentation in fMRI data**. My work focuses on model performance and validation within an interdisciplinary AI and neuroscience team.

### Cache Replacement Policies
**COEP Technological University**

Designed and implemented **FreqRec**, a hybrid LRU–LFU policy that combines recency and frequency signals for L1 data-cache replacement. Evaluated the policy using SPEC CPU 2017 benchmarks.

📄 **Publication:** *FreqRec: A Lightweight and Adaptive LFU-LRU Hybrid Policy for Cache Block Replacement* — IEEE CICN 2025  
[IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11367833) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/CICN67655.2025.11367833)
